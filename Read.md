# LinKod — A Mythic Linguistic & Symbolic System

**Description:**  
LinKod is a mythic linguistic system blending nature, emotion, ritual, and creation into a unified symbolic language. A living codex of forces, realms, lexicons, and cultural lore designed for storytelling, worldbuilding, and expressive programming.

---

## Overview

LinKod is a fully realized conlang and symbolic framework built on eight sacred forces, ritual syntax, ecological roots, emotional structures, and mythic cosmology. It serves as a cultural artifact, a storytelling engine, and a foundation for expressive computation.

This repository contains the complete LinKod Codex, including its language structure, mythic lore, lexicons, rituals, glyphs, and interpreter specifications.
